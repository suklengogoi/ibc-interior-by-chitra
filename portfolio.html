01 — The studio

Design that starts with
how you live.

IBC — Interior by Chitra is an interior design studio based in Tezpur, working on homes and commercial spaces across Assam. The work is built around one idea: a space should reflect the person using it, and it should be effortless to live in once the site team leaves.

Every project runs through the same sequence — understand the brief, plan the space, price it honestly, build it properly, and hand it over finished. Chitra stays involved from the first measured survey through to the last piece of hardware.

02 — Services

What the studio takes on.

01

Custom interior design

A complete scheme drawn around how you actually live — layouts, joinery, finishes, lighting and furniture chosen together rather than piece by piece.

02

Modular kitchen

Kitchens planned around the real work triangle, then built with the storage that suits your cooking, and finishes that survive an Assam summer.

03

Room makeovers

A single room brought up to the standard of the rest of the house, when a full renovation is not on the table.

04

Space planning

Circulation, storage volume, daylight and the small dimensional decisions that make a compact home feel larger.

05

Furniture & décor selection

Sourcing and specifying the loose pieces, lighting and textiles so the finished room reads as one considered whole.

03 — Process

Five stages, and no surprises.

01

Planning & design

Understand your vision, assess the space, and develop a design that balances how the room looks, how it works and how you live in it.

02

Cost estimation

A transparent, itemised estimate covering materials and execution, kept inside the budget you have planned for.

03

Building phase

The approved design brought to life through coordinated execution and careful craftsmanship.

04

Final touches

Finishes, furnishings, lighting and decorative pieces that pull the whole room together.

05

Project completion

Final inspections, remaining work resolved, and the space handed over ready to be lived in.
