01 — The studio

Design that starts with
how you live.

IBC — Interior by Chitra is an interior design studio based in Tezpur, working on homes and commercial spaces across Assam. The work is built around one idea: a space should reflect the person using it, and it should be effortless to live in once the site team leaves.

Every project runs through the same sequence — understand the brief, plan the space, price it honestly, build it properly, and hand it over finished. Chitra Ranjan Neog leads the studio and stays involved from the first measured survey through to the last piece of hardware.

04 — Services

What the studio
takes on.

A complete scheme drawn around how you actually live — layouts, joinery, finishes, lighting and furniture chosen together rather than piece by piece.

Residential and commercial · concept to handover

Kitchens planned around the real work triangle, then built with the storage that suits your cooking — tall pantry pull-outs, corner units, under-cabinet light and finishes that survive an Assam summer.

Layout · joinery · hardware · counters

05 — Process

Five stages, and
no surprises.

01

Planning & design

We start by understanding your vision, assessing the space, and developing a design that balances how the room looks, how it works and how you live in it.

02

Cost estimation

A transparent, itemised estimate covering materials, execution and the rest of the project requirements, kept inside the budget you have planned for.

03

Building phase

The approved design is brought to life through coordinated execution, careful craftsmanship and close attention to the details that are easy to skip.

04

Final touches

The space is refined with the finishes, furnishings, lighting and decorative pieces that pull the whole room together.

05

Project completion

Final inspections, any remaining finishing work resolved, and the space handed over ready to be lived in.
